Oasis sound license (version 1, 6 October 2026)

Plain-language terms for every sound program licensed on Oasis, alone or in a kit. They follow the answers that
established sound stores give buyers (Splice Sounds, the Sonniss GDC bundle): royalty-free, perpetual, no credit
required, no reselling the raw sounds.

What you get
- A license to use each program you paid for, at any knob setting and any seed, and every take it renders
  (including the WAVs in a game pack), in things you make.

You may
- Use the sounds in commercial and non-commercial work: games, apps, websites, videos, films, podcasts, music,
  ads and live performance, on any platform, worldwide, with no further fees or royalties.
- Edit, layer, process and mix them.
- Commit the rendered WAVs to a repository, public or private, when they ship as part of your project.
- Keep using them forever once the order's 14-day refund window has closed.

You may not
- Resell, share or give away the sounds or the programs on their own, as a sound library, sample pack, preset
  pack, asset pack or similar, or upload them to another sound marketplace.
- Use them to train or fine-tune machine-learning models.
- Publish your licensed module URL (the ?lic= link) in public code; it is your license key. Ship the WAVs instead,
  or keep the import on a server you control.

Credit
- Not required. If you want to credit the creators, the game pack's LICENSE.txt has a suggested line.

Who holds the license
- The buyer whose PayPal order paid for it (or whose agent's budget did). Someone else using the same kit buys
  their own copy.

Refunds
- An order can be refunded in full within 14 days; a full refund ends its licenses, and the module URLs and clean
  downloads stop working. A refund of parts billed twice keeps the licenses.

Creators
- Each creator keeps ownership of their programs and grants this license through Oasis. Their share of every
  order is booked when PayPal completes it and paid out after the refund window.

This is a plain-language license written for a hackathon project in the PayPal sandbox; no real money moves here.
